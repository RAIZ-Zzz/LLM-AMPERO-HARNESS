# Stairway to Heaven (studio, Led Zeppelin IV) — guitar solo

Audit rule: a claim is kept only if the quoted sentence was found on the opened page (2026-09-28).

## Kept
| Claim | Quote | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Guitar: Fender Telecaster | "I basically got my [Fender] Telecaster out to do that solo. Even though I'd been playing [Gibson] Les Pauls, I wanted to get that bite of the Telecaster on it." (Page) | https://www.loudersound.com/bands-artists/jimmy-page-stairway-to-heaven-guitar-solo-interview |
| 1959 Tele from Jeff Beck | "played for the recording on a 1959 Fender Telecaster given to Page by Jeff Beck" | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stairway_to_Heaven |
| Amp: Supro (contested) | "plugged into a Supro amplifier, although in an interview he gave to Guitar World magazine, Page said, 'It could have been a Marshall, but I can't remember'." | https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stairway_to_Heaven |
| Amp: Supro (Page quote, original source not named) | "The 'Stairway To Heaven' solo was done when I pulled out the Telecaster, which I hadn't used for a long time, plugged it into the Supro, and away it went again." | https://www.groundguitar.com/jimmy-page-gear/jimmy-pages-supro-coronado-1690t-amplifier/ |
| Supro speaker: single 12" | "Jimmy's amp originally had two 10-inch speakers, and when it was repaired in the '60s they changed it to a single 12." | same Ground Guitar page |
| Studio | "Page then returned to Island Studios to record his solo." | Wikipedia |

## Rejected / not found
- "No pedal, just guitar and amp" — came from a search summary attributed to Louder; not found on the opened page.
- Pickup position — not stated by any opened source.
- Any delay/reverb/echo on the solo — not stated by any opened source.
- Ground Guitar calls the amp both "Supro Coronado 1690T" (title) and "Supro Thunderbolt" (body): model is inconsistent within the source.
